Coffee Surfer Privacy Policy

Operator: Yunjo Kim · Contact: ggkimyj@gmail.com · Effective: October 16, 2026

1. What is stored, and where

Coffee Surfer (“the app”) has no sign-up or login and sends no information to the operator’s servers. The following is stored only on your device:

  • Drink log: drink name, caffeine amount (or unknown), time, and how much you drank
  • Settings: age group, weight, caffeine sensitivity, bedtime, app settings such as theme and sound, favorites
  • Check-ins: the dates you tapped “No coffee today”

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Dynamic Island (iPhone) use a shared storage area on the same device to show the time left and the scene. This information does not leave your device.

2. Health app (HealthKit) sleep data

Only if you turn it on, the app reads sleep analysis data from the iOS Health app. It is used only to show a summary on screen; it is not saved in the app’s storage or backup files, not used for ads or any other purpose, and not shared with anyone. The app does not write to the Health app.

3. Motion sensor

The app reads the device’s motion sensor so the coffee on screen sways when you tilt your phone. It is used only to move the picture and is neither stored nor sent.

4. Notifications

The bedtime reminder is off by default; notification permission is requested only when you turn it on. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push server is used.

5. Backup files

When you choose to export, your log, settings and check-in dates are written to a file (JSON or CSV) and handled by the sharing or saving method you pick. From then on, that file is kept under the policy of the service you chose.

6. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads are requested only for users who chose the “adult” age group, and only as non-personalized ads. The ads SDK may process device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and ad impression and click information to serve ads and prevent fraud. The app does not pass your drink log, weight, sensitivity or sleep data to the ads SDK.

Transfer abroad: while ads are shown, the information above may be sent to Google LLC (United States) to serve ads and prevent fraud, and is kept under Google’s policies. You can change your choice in the app under Settings › About me › “Ad privacy choices”; if you choose an age group other than adult, no ads are requested. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Retention and deletion

Information stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. In the app, Settings › About me › “Delete all data” removes your log, settings, favorites and check-in dates; removing the app also removes its data on the device. This does not apply to backup files you already exported or to information processed by the ads SDK. You can turn off notification, Health and motion permissions in your device settings at any time.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14. No ads are requested for users who chose the under-14 or teen age group.

9. About the numbers

The caffeine amounts and times in the app are estimates from what you enter and a general formula. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or judge safety.

10. Contact and changes

For questions or requests to access or delete personal information, write to ggkimyj@gmail.com. Person responsible for privacy: Yunjo Kim (ggkimyj@gmail.com). Because the app keeps no personal information on the operator’s servers, you can delete the information on your device yourself as described in section 7.

If this policy changes, this page will show the change with a new effective date.
