Frequently asked questions

Is there a login?
No. Your log is stored only on this device.

How do I move my log to a new phone?
Export a backup file from Settings › About me, then import it on the new phone.

The caffeine amount differs from the store menu.
The app uses the values published by each franchise. Let us know about a changed value and we will check it. For drinks not on the list, you can enter the caffeine amount yourself.

Are the times and amounts on screen exact?
They are estimates from what you enter and a general formula. They vary from person to person, and they are not medical advice or a diagnosis.

My widget doesn’t update.
Open the app once and it is redrawn. If it still doesn’t change, remove the widget and add it again.

How do I turn off the Dynamic Island?
Turn it off in the app under Settings › Dynamic Island.

How do I delete everything?
In the app, go to Settings › About me › Delete all data. Removing the app also removes its data on the device.
